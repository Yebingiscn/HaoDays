A conversation on board

Follow Alex and the flight attendant from the first meal choice to a polite thank-you.

  1. FFlight attendant01

    您好，吃什么？

    Nín hǎo, chī shénme?

    Hello, what would you like to eat?

  2. AAlex02

    有什么？

    Yǒu shénme?

    What's available?

  3. FFlight attendant03

    鸡肉饭还是牛肉面？

    Jīròu fàn háishi niúròu miàn?

    Chicken rice or beef noodles?

  4. AAlex04

    鸡肉饭吧，谢谢。

    Jīròu fàn ba, xièxie.

    I'll have the chicken rice, thanks.

  5. FFlight attendant05

    好的。您要喝什么？

    Hǎo de. Nín yào hē shénme?

    Sure. What would you like to drink?

  6. AAlex06

    有咖啡吗？

    Yǒu kāfēi ma?

    Do you have coffee?

  7. FFlight attendant07

    有的。

    Yǒu de.

    Yes, we do.

  8. AAlex08

    我要咖啡，谢谢。

    Wǒ yào kāfēi, xièxie.

    I'll have coffee, thank you.

  9. FFlight attendant09

    要加冰吗？

    Yào jiā bīng ma?

    Would you like ice in it?

  10. AAlex10

    要，谢谢。

    Yào, xièxie.

    Yes, please.

  11. FFlight attendant11

    好的，给您。

    Hǎo de, gěi nín.

    Sure, here you go.

  12. The flight attendant hands Alex his coffee.
  13. AAlex12

    有毯子吗？

    Yǒu tǎnzi ma?

    Do you have a blanket?

  14. FFlight attendant13

    有，稍等一下。

    Yǒu, shāo děng yíxià.

    Yes, just a moment.

  15. The flight attendant brings a blanket.
  16. FFlight attendant14

    给您。

    Gěi nín.

    Here you go.

  17. AAlex15

    谢谢！

    Xièxie!

    Thank you!

  18. FFlight attendant16

    不客气。

    Bú kèqi.

    You're welcome.

Two patterns to keep

Pattern 01

Ask if something is available

有 + 物品 + 吗？

Put the item between 有 and 吗. The final 吗 turns the sentence into a yes-or-no question.

有咖啡吗？

Yǒu kāfēi ma?

Do you have coffee?

有水吗？

Yǒu shuǐ ma?

Do you have water?

有毯子吗？

Yǒu tǎnzi ma?

Do you have a blanket?

Pattern 02

Say what you would like

我要 + 物品

Use 我要 to make a simple request. Add 谢谢 to make it friendly and polite.

我要咖啡，谢谢。

Wǒ yào kāfēi, xièxie.

I'd like coffee, thank you.

我要茶，谢谢。

Wǒ yào chá, xièxie.

I'd like tea, thank you.

我要牛肉面，谢谢。

Wǒ yào niúròu miàn, xièxie.

I'd like beef noodles, thank you.

You will also hear…

鸡肉饭吧。

Jīròu fàn ba.

I'll go with the chicken rice.

吧 softens a choice. You can answer with just the meal plus 吧.

加冰。 / 不加冰。

Jiā bīng. / Bù jiā bīng.

With ice. / No ice.

冰 (bīng) means ice. Use 不 before 加冰 when you do not want any.

要，谢谢。 / 不要，谢谢。

Yào, xièxie. / Bú yào, xièxie.

Yes, please. / No, thank you.

Short answers are natural. 不用，谢谢 (Bú yòng, xièxie) is another polite way to decline.

给您。

Gěi nín.

Here you go.

Listen for this when someone hands you a drink, a meal or a blanket.

稍等一下。

Shāo děng yíxià.

Just a moment.

A common reply when someone is getting something for you.
