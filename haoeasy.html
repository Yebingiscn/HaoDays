Guide 01 / Mobile payments

Alipay and WeChat Pay

Set up one payment app before you reach the counter. Overseas card support and verification depend on the app and your card issuer.

  1. Install and sign in

    Download Alipay (支付宝) or WeChat (微信) from your phone’s app store. Register with a number that can receive verification codes.

  2. Add a supported bank card

    Find the bank-card section in Alipay or in WeChat Pay. Enter your card details and complete any identity or issuer checks requested by the app.

  3. Check the amount and pay

    Scan the merchant’s QR code (扫一扫) or open your own payment code for the cashier to scan. Check the payee and amount before confirming.

Guide 02 / Train tickets

Railway 12306

China Railway’s 12306 service is the official place to book a train. Keep the same passport with you when you travel.

  1. Create a passenger profile

    Use the English 12306 site or the official Railway 12306 app. Enter the passenger’s name and passport details as shown on the document and follow the verification prompts.

  2. Check the exact station

    Search by date and city. Read the full station name before paying, especially when a city has several stations with compass directions in their names.

  3. Bring your passport to the station

    Keep your train and seat details handy. Use the passport linked to your ticket for the identity check. Ask for the staffed lane if the gate cannot read it.

Guide 03 / Getting around

Book a ride with DiDi

Use the DiDi Greater China app or the ride-hailing service inside a supported payment app. Keep your destination’s Chinese address handy.

  1. Pin the right pickup spot

    Choose a spot you can recognize in person, such as a hotel entrance or a numbered station exit.

  2. Confirm your destination

    Compare the address in the app with the one from your hotel or venue. Review the pickup point and the fare shown in the app before booking.

  3. Match the license plate

    Check the car’s plate against the booking before you get in. Keep the trip screen open so you can check the route.
