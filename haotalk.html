Lesson 01 / At the counter

Order a bowl of noodles

Start with what you want, ask for a little heat, and find out whether you can scan to pay. Listen to the Mandarin and repeat each line.

Read the transcript
  • 你好，我想要一碗面。 · Nǐ hǎo, wǒ xiǎng yào yì wǎn miàn. · Hello, I’d like a bowl of noodles.
  • 微辣，谢谢。 · Wēilà, xièxie. · Mildly spicy, please.
  • 可以扫码吗？ · Kěyǐ sǎomǎ ma? · Can I scan the code?

Lesson 02 / Getting around

Find the metro

Use a polite opener to ask for the nearest station, then listen for the directions you might hear back.

Read the transcript
  • 请问，地铁站在哪儿？ · Qǐngwèn, dìtiězhàn zài nǎr? · Excuse me, where’s the metro station?
  • 一直走，然后左转。 · Yìzhí zǒu, ránhòu zuǒ zhuǎn. · Go straight, then turn left.
  • 谢谢！ · Xièxie! · Thank you!
