City guide / 成都

One day in Chengdu

Leave time to sit down for tea. This three-stop plan takes you through central Chengdu, with a break for noodles or dumplings along the way.

  1. Morning · People’s Park 人民公园

    Walk the park paths and settle into a teahouse for a cup of jasmine tea.

  2. Midday · Kuanzhai Alleys 宽窄巷子

    Explore the courtyards and side lanes, then look for a bowl of dandan noodles nearby.

  3. Late afternoon · Wuhou Shrine and Jinli 武侯祠与锦里

    Visit the shrine if it’s open, then head to nearby Jinli for snacks and a walk past the lanterns.

At the table / 成都美食

Three things to order

Chengdu is known for Sichuan cooking. Look for these names on a menu, and try 微辣 (wēilà) if you’d like a milder order.

Dandan noodles

担担面

Noodles tossed with a savory, often spicy sauce.

Mapo tofu

麻婆豆腐

Soft tofu in a chili-bean sauce with Sichuan pepper.

Red oil wontons

红油抄手

Delicate wontons dressed with fragrant chili oil.
