Short film 01 / 茶

Tea as a welcome

A small gesture with a long history. Listen for the words a host might use when offering you a cup of tea.

Read the transcript
  • 客人来了，先倒杯茶。 · When a guest arrives, a cup of tea can be a welcome.
  • 在茶馆里，喝茶也是聊天的好机会。 · At a teahouse, sharing tea is a chance to talk.
  • 请喝茶。 · Please have some tea.

Short film 02 / 春节

The Spring Festival table

Hear how families mark the new year together and learn a greeting to share at the table.

Read the transcript
  • 春节，家人常常聚在一起。 · At Spring Festival, families often get together.
  • 除夕夜，一家人一起吃年夜饭。 · On New Year’s Eve, families gather for a reunion dinner.
  • 见面时，可以说：新年快乐！ · When you greet someone, you can say “Happy New Year!”
